About

Bansi Patel, CFA is an investor with experience across public credit, private credit, fixed income portfolio management, private equity underwriting, and fundamental equity research. He has held investment roles at MetLife Investment Management and Polen Capital Credit, advised private equity clients on diligence and value creation at Strategy&, and currently evaluates lower-middle-market acquisition opportunities through Center State Capital Partners.

He holds an MBA from NYU Stern School of Business, where he served as a portfolio manager for the Michael Price Student Investment Fund and was a Teaching Fellow to Professor Aswath Damodaran in Valuation. He is also a CFA charterholder.

His investment process emphasizes downside protection, cash-flow durability, capital structure, valuation, and the relationship between business quality and price, with particular attention to scenarios where market expectations diverge from underlying economics.

The research published on this site reflects independent investment analysis and the author’s own views.
